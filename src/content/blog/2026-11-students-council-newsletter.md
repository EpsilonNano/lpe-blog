Welcome to the very first issue of the Students' Council newsletter (and a revival of *The Trojan*)! Let's quickly run through what we did for the first two months of the school:

### Senior Sunrise

Following a massive success of last year's senior sunrise (organized by Ella and Abbi), we decided to continue on this tradition too! Our seniors gathered on turf for a fun and exciting sunrise together, sharing some treats and taking a nice group photo right as the sun peeked through the horizon.

### Grade 9 Night

Welcoming our very newest students, our Grade 9 Night was fun activity-filled evening in the gym where we played a variety of entertaining large-scale games designed to bring people together and break the ice.

### Ice Cream Truck

We got an ice cream truck to come at lunch to serve out some delicious frozen treats!

### Homecoming

The usual flagship night did not disappoint! The crowd was packed; the food, from *Chef Hotdog*, was amazingly delicious, and the carnival-style games (inspired again by last year's Homecoming) were a massive hit. From the grade 9s which had barely spent a few weeks at the school to the seniors which have spent their entire highschool careers here, we had a lot of everyone show up and enjoy the festivities.

### Spirit Week

We also had a super fun and exciting spirit week! From Twin Day to the Pink-out, Minion Day and a Pep Rally, it was jam packed with activities to show off our school spirit! The Pep Rally was especially of note, as we had our very own Ethan Lu and Emily McKnight announcing the electric students vs teachers football game along with many other small scale games with prizes.

### Soak a Senior

Wednesday October 21st marked a very splashing (and wet) day for Liam McDermott, Ethan Lu, Soleil Brooks, Ravreet Sharia, and Luke Mitchell as they got drenched! Participants donated money to breast cancer research in order to raise awareness and funds for the cause, and as a way to thank them, they got to splash our seniors with (perhaps too much?) water!

### Halloween

While Halloween fell on a Saturday this year, that did not stop us from dabbling in some holiday fun! On Wednesday the 28th, we hosted a haunted house that spanned nearly the whole school and led to many frightening jumpscares and creative costumes. And on Friday, we hosted the annual *North Park Costume Contest*, where the *80s* won the teacher category and many creative costumes won the many student categories and were given plenty of chocolate as compensation.

### The Future

We've had an absolute blast hosting so many events for you all, and a huge thanks goes out to all of the volunteers and participants who made this happen! We really could not do it without you guys, so from the bottom of our hearts, **thank you so much!** Looking forward, we have plenty of fun planned in store, including a lovely and skilled *Talent Show* coming up in the first week(s) of November!

Thank you for reading!
